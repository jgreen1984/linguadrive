Set up before you set off. Choose what to practise and tap a mode while parked. After that it's all spoken: answer out loud, and say skip, repeat, pause or stop. Steering-wheel play/pause and next buttons work too. Mount your phone and don't look at or touch it while driving.

Voice commands

  • “Skip” or “I don't know”: hear the answer and move on
  • “Repeat” or “Again”: hear the question again
  • “Pause”, then “Resume”: take a break
  • “Slower” / “Faster”: change the speaking speed
  • “Stop”: end the session and hear your score
  • Say nothing and you'll hear the answer after a few seconds.
  • Media buttons: play/pause pauses, next skips, previous repeats.
